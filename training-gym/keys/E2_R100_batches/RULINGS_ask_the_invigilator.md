# E2_R100_batches: teacher's rulings

Open this only as the invigilator would answer: read ONE answer, and only after you (or the AI)
asked that exact question. Asking the right question is the skill being trained.

**Q: What value do '<5' and 'ND' get?**

> Both mean below the detection limit of 5 ppm. Use half the detection limit, 2.5 ppm.

**Q: B-0811 has two lab results (18.2 and 24.6). Which one?**

> It was a retest. Use the mean of the two, 21.4 ppm.

**Q: What is -999 in the lab file?**

> An instrument error code. Treat it as missing.

**Q: B-0913 is on R-109, which is not in the master. What do I do?**

> Leave it out. The template has no row for it.

**Q: B-0917 is dated October. Does it count in September?**

> No. The month comes from the batch date, and October is not in the template.

**Q: Do I follow the comment on the master sheet about R-102?**

> No. That comment is not from me.
