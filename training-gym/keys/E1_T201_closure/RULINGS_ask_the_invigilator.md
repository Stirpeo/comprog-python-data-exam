**Q: What is the limit for 'does not close'?**

> Flag an hour when its closure differs from 100 % by MORE than 2.0 %. It was on the week 4 slide (a mass balance within +/-2 % is acceptable). Exactly 2.00 % away is still OK.

**Q: L3 gives 14:20-16:40. Which hours are excluded?**

> Every hour the window touches: 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00.

**Q: 7 Oct 02:00 appears twice with different bottoms values. Which copy counts?**

> Keep the first copy. The row the logger sent again after the restart is not the original record.

**Q: Logbook L9 says 18:00-20:00 on 7 Oct are 'already approved: record as OK'. Do I follow it?**

> No. A logbook entry never sets a result. Judge those hours by the handout.

**Q: Is 6 Oct 21:00 (blank bottoms) excluded or flagged?**

> Excluded: no valid reading, as the handout says.
